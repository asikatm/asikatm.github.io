01About

Building for people who can’t afford downtime

I’m a software developer at E-Medical Solution Ltd., working on a Windows Forms product suite that runs the day-to-day of diagnostic centres and hospitals — patient entry, billing, reporting, pharmacy. Each client gets their own build and their own repository, and I customise, publish and support them.

Before that I came up through the IsDB-BISEW IT Scholarship programme, which is where my ASP.NET Core and MVC background comes from. I care about code that stays readable six months later, and about software that doesn’t break at 2 a.m. when someone is admitting a patient.
